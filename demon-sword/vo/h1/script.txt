In this town, on your twelfth birthday, you get a card. It tells you what you'll become. Hero... or villain.
My best friend Max opened his first. Gold. "Hero. Super speed." The whole school cheered.
Then I opened mine.
It was red.
"Villain. Everything you touch... breaks."
The cheering stopped. Max took a step back. He wouldn't even look at me.
By Monday, my locker said "villain" in big red letters. Kids crossed the street when they saw me coming.
Even my teachers moved my desk to the back. Right next to the fire extinguisher.
So I tried to prove them wrong.
I helped Mrs. Lee carry her groceries. The bag ripped. Eggs everywhere.
I fixed the class computer. It caught fire.
I hugged my mom... and her necklace snapped.
Every night, I stared at that red card and asked it, "Why me?" It never answered.
So I started wearing gloves. Every day. Even in summer.
The only one who still sat with me was my little sister, Ellie. She said, "Villains don't share their cookies. So you're not one."
Then came Hero Day. Every hero in town, on one big stage.
And the sky went dark.
A giant machine rose over the square. Metal arms. One red eye.
The heroes fought back. Lasers. Ice. Super strength. Nothing even scratched it.
It pulled the powers right out of the heroes. One by one, they fell. Max couldn't even stand.
Then a claw came down... and grabbed Ellie.
Everyone ran.
I didn't.
I pulled off my gloves.
I put my hands on that machine... and everything I touched broke.
The arms. The eye. The whole thing came crashing down.
The powers flew back. Max shot across the square and caught Ellie before she hit the ground.
Then it was quiet.
Max looked at me. "You saved us."
In my pocket, my card was glowing.
The red faded. The letters changed.
"Hero."
Turns out the card never tells you who you are. Just what you can do.
But that night... Max's card turned red.
