In my town, mirrors are against the law. If you break the rule... you disappear.
Every window is painted black. The lake is covered with boards. Even our spoons are made of wood.
Grandma says it keeps me safe. "Never look at your reflection, Sora. Promise me."
And the fog never leaves. The road out of town just ends in white. Nobody ever leaves. Nobody ever comes.
But my town is strange.
Grandma cooks dinner every night. But I'm the only one who eats.
Mister Oda at the shop gives me candy every day. He has never taken my money. Not once.
My best friend Mina never lets me hold her hand. She just smiles and says, "Not today."
At night, under the street lamps, I'm the only one with a shadow. Grandma says the fog eats them.
Behind the shrine, there's a field of stones, all with the same date. Nobody will tell me what happened that day.
And when it rains, everyone stays dry. Everyone... except me.
One morning, something was shining in the river. The water was so quiet. Even the crows stopped.
A piece of broken mirror.
My hands were shaking. If I looked, I would disappear. That's what they always told me.
But I had to know. So I picked it up... and I looked.
I saw myself. My face. My hair. Me.
But behind me, the street was empty.
No Grandma. No Mina. No Mister Oda. Nobody.
They were right. When you look in a mirror, someone disappears. It just wasn't me.
I turned around. They were all standing there, glowing blue in the fog.
Grandma stepped forward. "Twelve years ago, the night you were born, there was a fire," she whispered. "The whole town burned. Only one baby came out alive."
"You."
"We couldn't leave you alone. So we stayed. We raised you. And we hid the mirrors, so you would never know."
Mina was crying. "I wanted to hold your hand so many times," she said.
Every face I ever knew. Every voice. They were gone before I ever said my first word.
Grandma held my hand... and for the first time, I couldn't feel it.
"We were always here," she said. "And we always will be."
