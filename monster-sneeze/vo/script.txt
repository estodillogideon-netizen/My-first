In my town, every kid has a monster under the bed.
And every monster has one weakness. Laughter.
One giggle, and we sneeze. Two giggles, and we itch all over.
I'm Bo. And I live under Lily. The giggliest kid in town.
Lily laughs at everything. Cartoons. Pancakes. Her own feet.
And every time she laughs, I sneeze so hard the whole bed bounces.
I tried earmuffs. I tried a tiny gas mask. I even slept in the sock drawer.
Nothing worked.
Every night, the monsters on our street meet in the Underbed.
Grizzle lives under a kid who never smiles. He sleeps like a baby.
Pip lives under a kid who only snores. She has never sneezed once.
And me? They call me Sneezy Bo.
"Sneezy Bo got the giggle kid!" they say. Every single night.
All I wanted was one quiet night.
Then one morning, a big truck parked next door.
Lily's best friend, Rosie, was moving away.
Lily waved until the truck was gone. She didn't laugh. Not once.
That night, no giggles. No sneezes. Just quiet.
The next night, quiet again. And the next. A whole week of quiet.
I finally got what I wanted. So why did it feel so wrong?
Then I heard it. A tiny sound from above. Lily was crying under her blanket.
And I realized something. I missed her laugh.
I missed sneezing. I even missed the bouncing bed.
So I did the craziest thing a monster can do.
I decided to make a kid laugh. On purpose.
Night one, I left funny drawings by her pillow. A cat in a bow tie. A pickle with a mustache.
She smiled. Almost.
Night two, I made her socks dance across the floor.
She peeked. But she didn't laugh.
Night three, I went all in. I borrowed her mom's curly wig. I put a sock on my horn. And I danced.
Lily lifted the blanket. And she saw me.
A big, purple, fluffy monster. In a wig.
For a second, nothing happened.
Then she laughed. The biggest laugh of her whole life.
And I sneezed so hard, I flew right up and bonked the ceiling.
Now we were both laughing. Well, she was laughing. I was sneezing.
"You're my monster?" she whispered. I nodded. My nose was very red.
Now Lily laughs every night again. And I sneeze every night again.
The other monsters still call me Sneezy Bo. I don't mind.
Because every night, there's a box of tissues under the bed. With a note. "For Bo."
Turns out, some things are worth sneezing for.
